# Code of Conduct

ADARA AI LAB is a professional research and engineering community.

## Expected

- Respect contributors, annotators, and the communities whose languages we work with
- Credit data sources, speakers, and researchers
- Assume good faith; disagree on ideas, not people

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, discrimination, or extraction of community data without consent
- Publishing private security issues
- Committing secrets or private datasets

## Enforcement

Report to info@adara.ai. Maintainers may warn, restrict, or remove access.
